📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Adidas sales performance using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into sales trends, product performance, regional sales distribution, and retailer performance.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that help understand sales performance and support data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze overall Adidas sales performance.

Identify top-performing products and product categories.

Understand sales distribution across different regions.

Evaluate retailer performance.

Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.

Identify key revenue-generating sales methods.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization.

Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and preparation.

Power Query – Data transformation.

DAX – Calculated measures and business metrics.

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Sales

Total Profit

Total Units Sold

Operating Profit

Average Selling Price

Sales Growth

📈 Dashboard Features

Sales Overview: Provides an overview of total sales, profit, and units sold.

Product Analysis: Identifies products contributing to overall sales performance.

Regional Analysis: Compares sales performance across different regions.

Retailer Analysis: Evaluates sales contributions from different retailers.

Sales Method Analysis: Compares sales through different distribution channels.

Time-Based Analysis: Highlights monthly and yearly sales trends.

💡 Business Insights

The dashboard helps businesses:

Identify high-performing products and regions.

Understand retailer contributions to revenue.

Monitor profitability and sales performance.

Compare different sales channels.

Make informed business decisions using interactive visualizations.
